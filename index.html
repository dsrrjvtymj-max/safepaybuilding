How It Works

From contract to completion in four steps

  1. 1

    Create the project

    Define the scope of work with your contractor and break it into milestones with agreed-upon amounts.

  2. 2

    Fund the escrow

    The client deposits the full project amount into a secure SafePay escrow account before work begins.

  3. 3

    Complete & verify milestones

    As each milestone is finished, the contractor submits proof of work for the client to review and approve.

  4. 4

    Funds are released

    Approved milestones trigger an automatic, instant release of that portion of the funds to the contractor.
