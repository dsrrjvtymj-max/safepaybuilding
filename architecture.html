How It's Built

Powered by MangoPay's regulated wallet infrastructure

SafePay Building never touches client money directly. Every wallet, payment and payout runs through Mangopay, an FCA-authorised Electronic Money Institution1, with funds ultimately held in a segregated Barclays e-money account — separate from SafePay's own operating funds.

Users & KYC

Every Customer, Contractor and Supplier is onboarded as a Mangopay User — an individual or a business. Before anyone can hold a live wallet balance or request a payout, they have to clear Mangopay's KYC/KYB checks: proof of identity, proof of address, and for businesses, registration and beneficial-owner documentation2. SafePay Building collects this during sign-up and hands it to Mangopay's API — we never store the documents ourselves.

Wallets

Each onboarded user gets their own Mangopay e-wallet. Wallets can receive funds (a Payin), hold a balance, move money to another wallet on the platform (a Transfer), and send money out to a real bank account (a Payout)3. SafePay Building's dashboards are really just a purpose-built view onto these four actions, tailored to construction projects.

Escrow, via milestone-linked transfers

SafePay Building doesn't move money the moment work is marked done. When a customer funds a project, that money sits in their own wallet — not the contractor's — until a milestone is approved. Approval is what triggers a Mangopay Transfer moving the agreed amount into the contractor's wallet. If a milestone is disputed, the transfer simply doesn't fire until the case is resolved.

Linked suppliers — the wallet network effect

Because every party sits inside the same wallet infrastructure, money doesn't have to leave the platform to move between them. A contractor who needs to pay a plasterer, a timber yard, or any other supplier that's also set up on SafePay Building can send a wallet-to-wallet Transfer directly — no card fees, no exchanging bank details, and the payment stays traceable back to the original project. Suppliers get a lightweight version of the wallet dashboard: incoming payments, balance, and a payout button — without any of the project or milestone tooling a contractor needs.

Payouts to real bank accounts

When a contractor or supplier is ready to withdraw, Mangopay pays out from their wallet to a UK bank account they've registered as a Recipient. Domestic payouts typically arrive within seconds over Faster Payments; international payouts route over SWIFT and can take up to four business days3.
