What happens if a milestone is disputed

Most projects never need this page. But if a customer and contractor disagree about whether a milestone is really finished, here's exactly what happens to the work — and the money.

The Process

Four steps, start to finish

  1. 1

    Raise a dispute

    Either party flags the milestone in question, from their dashboard, within 5 days of it being submitted for approval.

  2. 2

    Evidence review

    Both sides upload photos, messages and inspection reports. The disputed tranche stays frozen in escrow the whole time.

  3. 3

    Mediator decision

    A SafePay case mediator reviews everything submitted and proposes a resolution — typically within 3 business days.

  4. 4

    Funds released

    The disputed amount is released per the agreed outcome: fully to the contractor, fully back to the customer, or split.

What happens to the money while it's under review

Nothing moves. The disputed tranche sits exactly where it was before the dispute was raised — in the customer's escrow wallet — because SafePay Building only ever fires a Mangopay Transfer once a milestone is approved. A dispute simply means that approval hasn't happened yet. Every other milestone, approved or not yet reached, is completely unaffected: a contractor never loses access to funds already released for earlier, undisputed work.

The best way to avoid one

Almost every dispute traces back to a vague milestone description agreed at the start. See Breaking a Project into Milestone Tranches for how to write ones that are specific enough that both sides already agree what "done" looks like before work even begins.

  • Write milestone descriptions around a verifiable outcome, not a date or a percentage
  • Agree inspection criteria for each milestone before work starts on it
  • Take dated photos at the start and end of every stage
  • Raise concerns early — a quick message often resolves things before a formal dispute is needed
